Menzia Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Menzia is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform. We do not tolerate child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child exploitation, grooming, or any form of sexual abuse involving minors.

2. Zero-Tolerance Policy

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Menzia:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, or distributing CSAM.
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Grooming or attempting to exploit minors.
  • Sexualization of children in any form.
  • Soliciting sexual content from minors.
  • Any activity that endangers the safety of children.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can contact our child safety team to report suspected child exploitation, abuse, or child sexual abuse material.

Child safety concerns can be sent to: masumyt263@gmail.com

Please include relevant details so that we can investigate the report. Do not download, copy, or forward suspected illegal material.

4. Action Against Violations

When we become aware of violations of these standards, we will review the available information and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable laws and our platform policies.

Actions may include removing violating content, restricting accounts, or terminating accounts where appropriate.

5. Compliance with Applicable Laws

Menzia is committed to complying with applicable child safety and child protection laws.

Where legally required, confirmed child sexual abuse material will be reported to the appropriate authorities or designated organizations.

6. Age Restrictions

Menzia is intended for users who meet the minimum age requirements enforced by the app and applicable laws. Access by minors is subject to the age restrictions and safeguards implemented by Menzia.
