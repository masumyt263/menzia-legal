Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Welcome to Menzia. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Menzia collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Menzia is designed to support anonymous communication. We do not require a phone number or email address to create an account.

Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • Account information: A unique account identifier, username, display name and information needed for account recovery.
  • Profile information: Country, age, gender, interests and profile photo, where provided.
  • Chat information: Messages, message requests, conversation details and timestamps.
  • Matching information: Matching preferences, online status and activity timestamps needed for matching.
  • Technical information: Information processed by app service providers to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Display user profiles.
  • Deliver messages and message requests.
  • Operate matching and chat features.
  • Show online status and availability.
  • Maintain app security and prevent abuse.
  • Improve app performance and respond to support requests.

3. Storage and Third-Party Services

Menzia uses Google Firebase services for certain app functions. Depending on the features enabled, information may be processed through Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage and Firebase Cloud Functions.

These services help us operate and maintain the app. Their processing is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Some profile information may be visible to other users as part of the app's features. Messages are intended for the participants in the relevant conversation.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate, secure and maintain Menzia. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

5. Data Security

We use available technical and organizational measures to help protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Keep your account recovery key private. Do not share it with anyone.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Menzia account and associated data.

To request account deletion, visit our Account Deletion page or contact us at masumyt263@gmail.com .

Requests will be verified before processing. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Menzia is intended for adults aged 18 and above. It is not intended for children under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
