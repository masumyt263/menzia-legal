Request Account Deletion

Menzia users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Click the email button below.
  2. Enter your Menzia username in the email.
  3. Tell us that you want your account deleted.
  4. Send the request to our support team.

We may request additional information to verify account ownership. Never send your recovery key or password by email.

Request Account Deletion

What Happens After a Request?

We will review and verify your request before processing it. Account information and associated data will be deleted according to our deletion process and applicable legal requirements.

Some information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.
